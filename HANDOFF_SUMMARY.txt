3. PACKAGE 2: BACKEND DEEPFAKE DETECTION MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Directory: C:\Users\Ananya Jain\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\trustlens-deepfake-backend

What it adds to TRUSTLENS:
1. AUDIO DEEPFAKE & VOICE CLONE DETECTION (Primary Feature):
   - Pretrained Model: "garystafford/wav2vec2-deepfake-voice-detector" (Hugging Face)
     Alternative: "Bgeorge/deepfake-audio-detector-wav2vec2"
   - Architecture: Wav2Vec2-Base (~95M parameters, ~360 MB).
   - CPU-Friendly: Optimized to run on standard laptops without GPU in ~150ms-400ms.
   - Physical Acoustic Signal Analyzer: Computes genuine physical signal metrics:
     * High-frequency vocoder attenuation (>7.5 kHz cutoff)
     * Spectral flatness (Wiener entropy)
     * Fundamental pitch (F0) micro-jitter (human vocal fold jitter vs robotic TTS)
     * Digital zero silence in pauses (common in AI-generated voice clips)
   - Outputs:
     * synthetic_risk_score (0.0 to 1.0)
     * model_confidence (0.0 to 1.0, strictly distinguished from risk)
     * overall_assessment ("likely_authentic", "potentially_synthetic", "inconclusive")
     * Plain-language explanation + specific acoustic indicators detected.

2. VIDEO DEEPFAKE ANALYSIS (Extension Feature):
   - Accepts MP4, MOV, AVI, WEBM.
   - Extracts key frames using OpenCV and checks for temporal sharpness variance,
     face boundary blending artifacts, and static head animation anomalies.

3. PRIVACY & SECURITY SAFEGUARDS:
   - Ephemeral Processing: Files uploaded to temp storage are GUARANTEED deleted 
     immediately upon scan completion or failure (zero permanent media retention).
   - No Biometrics / Speaker ID: Analyzes synthetic voice characteristics only, never 
     attempts to identify the individual speaker.
   - Upload Guards: Enforces 25 MB file limit and 0.5s to 300s duration constraints.

4. VERIFICATION GUIDANCE ADVISOR:
   - Tailored counter-measures against CEO fraud, voice memo scams, and family emergency
     voice cloning attacks.
   - Returns out-of-band callback protocols and dual-authorization warnings.
